# Support

## Cold Email Reply Triage

For help, bug reports, or feature requests, open a GitHub issue in this repository:

https://github.com/eilte0528/Cold-Email-Reply-Triage.-/issues

When reporting a problem, include:

- What you were trying to do
- The result you expected
- What happened instead
- A small example, with sensitive information removed

Please do not post private email addresses, passwords, API keys, or other sensitive information in an issue.
