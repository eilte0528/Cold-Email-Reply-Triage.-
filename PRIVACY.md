# Privacy Policy

**Cold Email Reply Triage**

Last updated: October 7, 2026

Cold Email Reply Triage is a tool for classifying and organizing cold-email replies. It does not require a separate account with the developer.

## Information you provide

When you use the tool, you may provide email-reply text and related context. This information is used to perform the requested classification, prioritization, and drafting task.

## How information is used

Information provided in a request is used to generate the requested output. The developer does not sell user-provided content or use it for unrelated marketing purposes.

## Third-party services

The tool operates within ChatGPT and may be subject to OpenAI's applicable privacy practices and terms. The developer does not control ChatGPT's data handling.

## Data retention

The developer does not operate a separate database for email replies submitted through the tool. Do not submit sensitive personal information unless it is necessary for your task.

## Changes

This policy may be updated when the tool or its data practices change.

## Contact

For support or privacy questions, open an issue in this repository.
